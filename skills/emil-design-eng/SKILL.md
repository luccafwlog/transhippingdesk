# Design engineering

Apply Emil Kowalski's design-engineering principles to the requested component.
Preserve the project's tokens and interaction conventions. Choose motion for
feedback and orientation, accounting for use frequency and reduced-motion needs.
Treat timings and effects in the references as options to evaluate in context.

Read only the relevant reference:

- [animation.md](references/animation.md): deciding whether to animate, easing,
  duration and springs.
- [components.md](references/components.md): buttons, popovers, tooltips and
  interruptible transitions.
- [transforms-gestures.md](references/transforms-gestures.md): transform geometry,
  clipping, drag and pointer capture.
- [performance-accessibility.md](references/performance-accessibility.md):
  rendering cost, reduced motion and touch devices.
- [component-craft.md](references/component-craft.md): toast patterns, stagger
  and debugging motion.

Validate the changed interaction in its actual context. Explain material
before/after differences in the format that best serves the request.
Source: [Emil Kowalski's animation course](https://animations.dev/).
